### The site — verified on disk 2026-08-10

`ai-agents/knowledge-base/conventions/task-status-vocabulary.md:21`:

> `| **Moved** | `➡️ Moved to [Sprint N](…) — priority M` | Carried to another sprint. Not dead, not done — relocated. | Producer |`

`N` is undefined in that row. Today that is harmless — the parser at `dashboard.sh:692` only ever
matched digits, so `N` **was** a number. After `0264` lands ADR-040 §6's companion, a move target is a
**sprint identity**: `4`, or `4c`. A producer writing `➡️ Moved to [Sprint 4c]` is then correct, and a
reader of this convention has no way to know that from the row.
