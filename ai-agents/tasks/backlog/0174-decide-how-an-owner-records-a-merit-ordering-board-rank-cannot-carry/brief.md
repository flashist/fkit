### The defect itself

`/fkit-task-brief` step 5 forbids inserting into the owner's ranking: new work **appends**. A board
row can therefore only be re-ranked **within its own open segment** — the run of consecutive open rows
it sits in — because a closed row above it is a wall nothing may move past. As the board closes out,
those segments fragment, and rows become **unreachable**: their merit position is no longer
expressible as a rank at all.

**The mechanism is confirmed and worsening.** Report §6.2, measured 2026-08-01 (method: every board
row matching a `P<n>` cell; status read from field 1 of the pipe-split only; closed = the status cell
**starts with** `✅ Done`, `⛔ Cancelled` or `➡️ Moved`):

| Measure | 2026-07-30 (`0160`'s brief) | **2026-08-01 (the report)** |
|---|---|---|
| Board rows with a `P<n>` cell | 145 | **148** |
| Closed | 121 (83%) | **123 (83%)** |
| Open | 24 | **25** |
| Disjoint open segments | 6 | **6** |
| **Open rows unreachable from the append zone** | **11 of 24** | **17 of 25** |
| Singletons (can never move at all) | 2 | **1** |

**Unreachable open rows went 11 → 17 in two days.** The board is **83% closed**.
