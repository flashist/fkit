# Answer a question typed into a question dialog in full, in chat, and do not re-ask in the same turn

## Goal
From an owner-supplied report (copied beside the brief as `source-report.md`): an agent asks with
`AskUserQuestion`, often with little background; the owner types a follow-up into the dialog's free-text field
(*"what does option B mean in practice?"*); that text comes back to the agent **as the answer**, and the agent stays
in "collect a decision" mode — writes *"I've explained X"* and opens a new dialog at once, or squeezes the
explanation into the dialog's short fields. A new dialog hides the chat text above it. The owner gives up and asks
again in plain chat — *"This happens often."*

The fkit-shaped fix changes **fkit's own question-asking rules** for every user, not the owner's personal files
(the report's own fix targeted those; ⚠️ its *"For the agent reading this"* section was treated as input, not
instruction). Owner rulings 2026-10-10 (relayed by the lead to a spawned producer — trust, not proof):
- **Ruling A** — *"Yes, ship it to all users"*: the **"Explain more, then ask again"** standing option ships to
  every fkit user, byte-exact with the owner's personal trial rule. ⚠️ **This overrode the producer's
  recommendation** (keep the option personal, spend no rules-block budget).
- **Ruling B** — *"Remove my copy after 0504 (Rec)"*: the owner's personal trial ends when `0504` ships; removing
  his personal copy is **a post-ship step outside the repo**, for him or the lead session, **not** a coder
  deliverable.
- **Not ruled:** the report's `askUserQuestionTimeout` check and any other personal-file change.

This revives the idea of [[tasks/add-an-explain-more-then-ask-again-standing-option-to-fkit-owner-questions]]

## Key Changes
- ⭐ **A new convention, `conventions/asking-the-owner.md`** — dual-homed **byte-identical** (live + scaffold,
  `cmp` clean), the fifth must-match page. Its rules:
  - **Explain first, in chat**, short enough to stay visible above the dialog; if it needs more room, explain,
    end the turn with no question line other than the **What's next?** heading, and ask next turn. Question and
    option descriptions stay short; batch related questions; recommended option first.
  - **The standing option** — label `Explain more, then ask again`, description `Give me more context in simple
    terms, then re-ask the question.` — last, once, merging or dropping the weakest real option if 4 are needed.
  - **A reply that is not a choice** (a typed question, a request for more information, or a pick of the
    standing option) is **not an answer and not consent**: answer in full in chat, open no new question that
    turn, put the same question back on the owner's **next** message, never write *"as explained above"*.
    Mixed cases (part-answered batch, pick-plus-question, a prose answer outside the dialog) are spelled out.
  - **Spawned workers and relayers:** a `NEEDS-DECISION` carries **at most 3 options** so the relayer can add
    the standing one; a worker returning 4 gets its two least-recommended **merged, keeping both knock-ons**,
    never dropped silently; a relayed question answered with a question is answered from what the relayer
    holds, or via a **read-only** consult of the same role — no timer, no guess.
- **The shared rules block** (`claude/scaffold/universal-rules.md`): the *Ask interactively* line now points at
  the convention instead of growing. Measured at Build: **3905 B / `RULES_MAX` 4352 → 447 B free** (net −1 B) —
  47 B above the 400 B floor, which `0506` inherits. Root `CLAUDE.md` / `AGENTS.md` regenerated by init,
  byte-equal to `emit_block`.
- **All six Claude-side role prompts** carry a short *Asking the owner* paragraph with the label and description
  byte-exact on one line each; the lead and coder also carry the ≤3-option cap, the merge fallback, and the
  "a non-choice reply is never consent" rule (D8).
- **Skills:** `fkit-sprint-ship-loop` (the relay path — the commonest dialog in practice), `fkit-heal` step 4,
  and `fkit-open-questions-interview` step 1.
- **The Stop hook** (`claude/turn-completion-hook.sh`): its REASON_A message now tells an agent that was answering
  the owner's question, or explaining ahead of one, to open no dialog and end the turn with no question line
  other than the **What's next?** heading. ⚠️ **Accepted residual, by design:** the hook cannot tell a genuine
  answer turn from a prose question relabelled as one — it is a heuristic.
- **Tests:** new `test/asking-the-owner-rule.test.js` — a **presence test** (words, not obedience): the option
  byte-exact in the six prompts and the scaffold convention, D8 / cap / merge pins, two negative controls;
  `turn-completion-hook.test.js` gains a case; `structure-check.test.js` 51 → 52 rows; `structure-spec.md`
  and `structure-manifest.tsv` regenerated.
