- [[decisions/adr-033-task-movers-are-producer-only-reversing-adr-025]] — ⚠️ **Reverses ADR-025**: only `fkit-producer` may run the movers, **hook-enforced at any spawn depth**. Amends ADR-019 (coder self-close) and ADR-032 (orchestrator close). **Restores separation of the closing *identity*, not prevention.** Triggered by task 0108, whose own recommendation the owner overruled
- [[decisions/adr-035-a-mid-board-insertion-is-not-the-owner-ruled-re-rank-exception]] — **The owner-ruled re-rank exception permits moving an existing row within its own open run — NOT inserting a new row mid-board.** Forced by arithmetic, not policy: an insertion renumbers every row below it, so **on an interleaved board there is no mid-board insertion point that does not renumber a closed row**. ⚠️ **It had already happened** — task `0174`'s own filing renumbered **eight closed rows** under an explicit owner ruling, and **two live records say it didn't**. The producer recorded its authority in full and **still breached an absolute rule, because the check ran in the wrong direction**. Accepted cost: **16 of 29 open rows sit behind a closed wall** and cannot be reached
- [[decisions/adr-036-the-skill-ownership-site-inventory-is-a-declared-registry]] — The site inventory stops being **remembered** in a comment and becomes a **declared** module plus a completeness tripwire. Triggered by a mirror checklist that **failed three times** — including once when it was **followed precisely**. ⚠️ **Records NO site count, deliberately** — an earlier revision hard-coded one and the sweep behind it was short by 17 rows. ⚠️ **Revised three times on its accepted date**, each revision correcting the ADR's own assertion, including **two false mechanisms withdrawn with nothing put in their place**. **Its honest limit: it makes the INVENTORY mechanical, not the SWEEP** — 4 of 6 historical classes, **0 of ≥5 current defects**. **Grants no completeness licence, with no exception clause**
- [[decisions/adr-030-stop-hook-enforces-turn-completion-contract]] — A **second hook** (`Stop`) enforces interactive questions + a "What's next?" close, because the prose rule **demonstrably did not fire**. Larger blast radius than ADR-018's — it can stop a turn completing. **Built 2026-07-23** (task 0127); **Addendum — 2026-07-23**: check A's "no `AskUserQuestion` this turn" signal comes from a **PreToolUse marker**, not the transcript (transcript presupposition proved false in review)
- [[decisions/adr-037-a-skill-rule-binds-a-spawned-worker-unless-the-instruction-relays-an-owner-ruling]] — **A rule in the skill a spawned worker is running BINDS it against a contrary spawn instruction — unless the instruction NAMES an owner ruling on that exact point.** Skill rules get a place in the precedence ladder **for the first time**; generalizes `fkit-coder.md`'s declared-approval marker from **one skill and one role to every spawned worker**, and on the owner's Q2 ruling **binds the driver too**. Neither extreme was available: *"the skill rule always wins"* would have re-pointed a **frozen review ledger** the owner ruled must not move. ✅ **BOTH CLAUSES LANDED 2026-08-04/05** (`0190` worker-side, on an owner-signed `RULES_MAX` bump 4096 → **4352**; `0191` driver-side) — ⚠️ **but the driver-side clause reaches NO driver**: `.claude/` was never refreshed, measured at 296 lines with the clause absent. ⚠️ **The worker-side clause shipped *"on that point"*, not §1's *"that EXACT point"*** — an owner-ruled residual **covered by nothing downstream**. ⚠️ **§5's *"no mechanical enforcement, and none is possible"* is KNOWN TO OVERSTATE** — false of a driver-side **carry-fidelity proxy** — and **the ADR on disk still says the stronger thing**; task `0205` owes the note
- [[decisions/adr-038-a-loop-steps-role-is-fixed-by-the-skill-the-step-runs]] — **A loop step's role is fixed by the skill the step runs, not by the deliverable's author** — the Process-review step is always `@fkit-coder`. Closes the invocation axis ADR-037 left open; rejects widening `skills_for_role()` by name (the grant is total-or-absent, the premise reaches four of seven roles). **Prose plus a paired detector, not prevention** — a driver working a role by hand never reaches the ADR-018 gate; the ADR-033 honesty shape reused. Recorded 2026-08-06 (`0222`, Sprint 3 P4) with the four-way number sweep evidenced
- [[decisions/adr-034-a-review-ledger-closes-on-the-work-product-not-the-task-s-own-record]] — **When a stateful review ledger closes**: clean on the **swept work product**; own-record residuals (`brief.md` / `worklog.md` / ledger bookkeeping) are **recorded, not re-reviewed**. A work-product defect **still blocks**. ⚠️ The split is drawn **per site, not per file** — one file can be both surfaces. **Its accepted cost: a closing task's worklog may carry known low-severity defects and genuine finds are forgone** (`0159` closed with **two own-record residuals standing**). **Binds three roles' skills; edits none of them**
